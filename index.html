Personal Playing Card

Why:

When I was very young, I couldn’t pronounce L in my name, with it often coming out more like "weef", so I would introduce myself like this. To this day it is still how I introduce myself in loud spaces. No phrase on a card could represent me better than this.

Pixel Portrait of Susan Kare

🛠️ Adobe Illustrator ⏰ April 2nd, 2025

What:

A pixel art portrait of Susan Kare, inspired by her graph paper portrait of Steve Jobs, extensive work on the first Machintosh, and Solitare for Windows 3.0. I also explored various environments it could be placed into, like as the logo for a fictional game studio.

Architectural Shapes and Colors

🛠️ Adobe Illustrator ⏰ October 16th, 2024

What:

Architexture from around my campus, vector-illustrated based on photos of the campus church and various other buildings that complemented the diagonal lines of the center photo of my dorm.

Why:

My first year dorm room was at the end of a hallway that felt impossibly long. When building a grid of USF architecture, it felt necessary for the origin point of my work to be the long walk at the start and end of every day.
