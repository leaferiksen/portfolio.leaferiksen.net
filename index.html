Personal Playing Card

🛠️ Aseprite ⏰ September 12th, 2025

A self-introduction card with the phrase I would say to teach people my name. Visually inspired by the Legend of Zelda (NES) intro screen.

When I was very young, I couldn’t pronounce L in my name, with it often coming out more like "weef", so I would introduce myself like this. To this day it is still how I introduce myself in loud spaces. No phrase on a card could represent me better than this.

Pixel Portrait of Susan Kare

🛠️ Adobe Illustrator ⏰ April 2nd, 2025

A pixel art portrait of Susan Kare, inspired by her graph paper portrait of Steve Jobs, extensive work on the first Machintosh, and Solitare for Windows 3.0. I also explored various environments it could be placed into, like as the logo for a fictional game studio.

Kare's work at Apple shaped the products that introduced me to pixel art, iconography, and interface design. I jumped at the opportunity to pay an homage to her legacy.

Architectural Shapes and Colors

🛠️ Adobe Illustrator ⏰ October 16th, 2024

Architexture from around my campus, vector-illustrated based on photos of the campus church and various other buildings that complemented the diagonal lines of the center photo of my dorm.

about me

📄 Resume🤝 LinkedIn📭 Email Me

Hello! I am a designer currently studying at the University of San Francisco. I care deeply about raising the standard for style, structure and accessibility in user interface and layout design. I see this work as a responsibility as someone who cares deeply about the future of the open web, native application frameworks, books, posters, and signage.

I have experience prototyping apps in Figma and bringing them to life with SwiftUI, and building websites in HTML, CSS and JS. I also enjoy making animated interactive graphics with p5.js and building layouts for print in the Adobe and Affinity suites.

I am currently working as an iMac Lab Assistant and Design Tutor for USFCA’s Art & Architecture department, and as an intern at OnCourt Live, a college tennis software startup.

In my free time I enjoy playing Minecraft and Osu!, reading visual novels, and watching mecha anime. If you are in SF and think we could be good friends, let's meet up for coffee or tea sometime!

If you want to make something awesome with me, please reach out! I can work with you in SF during the school year and Seattle during summer and winter break.
